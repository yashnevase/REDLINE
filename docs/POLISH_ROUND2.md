Remove the chamfered/cut-corner plates, rivets and bevel-frame look everywhere. **New language = Spirit Call's:** soft **rounded-corner plates** (radius 12–16 px, pills for buttons), flat/gradient fills with a soft shadow and a thin light edge, clean cream/white text on dark plates, round ability buttons with strong icons, small accent colours (violet/orange) only for state. No bevels, no panel textures, no angled corners. Keep our icon set and condensed heading font. Apply to HUD, banners, pause, results, mission select, workshop, options, audio popover, tooltips. Parity pair vs Spirit Call HUD for owner approval.
## R2 Enemies — back to characterful, not brown balls
- Restore the **concept lineup look**: faceted rust-metal bodies with panel seams, bolts, gear emblem, steel saw rings/discs, glowing eyes, strong silhouettes. Material contrast: bright worn **steel** parts vs **rust** bodies; each role gets a **colour accent** (Sawlet orange eye + steel ring, Thrower amber barrel glow, Shieldsaw dark plank + yellow nails, Grinder deep red-brown + big steel discs, Whirler hot-orange arm tips) so types read instantly at a glance.
- **Health bars:** small rounded bar above each enemy, shown when damaged (fades after 2 s without damage), role-coloured, with a white "ghost" drain on damage; Shieldsaw shows an armour segment; elites/boss get larger bars with name. Never overlap numbers.
- **Visible damage states (3 stages by HP):** 100–66 % scratches; 66–33 % dents, cracks, flickering sparks from the wound, saw teeth chipped; <33 % smoking, dangling/loose parts, eye flicker, slower wobble. Rusty-blade hits throw orange sparks and rust flakes; chunks of armour fall off on heavy hits. Cheap (decals, instanced particles, vertex displacement), budget checked.
