# Chapter 1 first-time-player check

POLISH_ROUND3 handoff: first the owner plays15minutes using Default view and records notes against F1 palette, F2 flat world life/scars, F3 scale, F4 visible density and F5 text. Then run the3–5-person check below and share their answers. The owner gives the final accepted verdict after review; Codex does not supply human playtest answers.

Owner runs this with 3–5 people who have not played REDLINE. No coaching for the first minute. Give them the lobby, let them choose a mission, and note their device/input. Audio is optional. This script is prepared; automated tests do not count as human responses.

Ask these five questions after a mission or a few attempts:

1. What were you trying to achieve, and how could you tell you were making progress?
2. How would you beat Scrap-Rex? Which warning or opening did you understand or miss?
3. What did the upgrade you picked change? What made you choose it?
4. What do Q, E and X do, and when would you combine them?
5. What was most fun, and what was confusing, tiring or annoying?

Record their own words, time to first intentional dash/ability/upgrade, missed UI, accidental actions, and whether they found missions/workshop. Ask them to locate the Core in a crowded frame. Do not explain a missed feature until their answer is recorded. Repeat troublesome actions with keyboard, controller or touch as available. Physical-phone testing remains waived by the owner; this does not establish universal performance.

Use findings to adjust START tuning or clarity, retain before/after captures, and rerun deterministic and rendering checks. Owner approves feel/art only after seeing the results.
