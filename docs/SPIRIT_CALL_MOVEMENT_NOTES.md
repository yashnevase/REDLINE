# Spirit Call movement study · DEPTH Part A

Read locally: `ref/spirit-call/spirit-call-main/src/features/player.ts`, `src/presentation/animate.ts`, `src/config/tuning.ts` `src/presentation/actors.ts` and `src/content/characters.ts`. The snapshot has no licence; these notes describe principles only. No code/assets copied.

- Player control writes committed dash direction/distance/duration; a final partial step keeps displacement exact. Normal movement approaches a target velocity with a bounded vector delta. Acceleration3200 and deceleration4200 in that game's units, with character speeds about260–330, imply roughly80–103ms acceleration and62–79ms stopping. Units are not portable to REDLINE. Input grace is80ms in that reference; REDLINE's owner now requests immediate release-stop instead.
- Logical facing follows input when not held by an attack; animation reads facing separately from translation. A dash keeps its committed direction and can carry bounded exit velocity into normal movement. There is no player-facing low-pass constant in the control system: smoothing should not be invented as a copied value.
- Animation reads interpolated positions and world timings. Hit-stop supplies zero animation delta, attacks/windups are sampled to the simulation phase, and clips are fitted to their action durations. Dashes hold locomotion loops. These timing relationships supply clarity more than large body deformation.
- The player presentation also has a damped sway spring driven by velocity changes, with stop/turn dust and zero spring delta in hit-stop. REDLINE keeps that readable response in eyes/marks and skid particles, while removing the sphere lean required by the new brief.
- REDLINE adaptation: vector-limited80ms acceleration, straight-line momentum8.5→10.5u/s over1s, a short turn drift and immediate no-input stop. The rigid sphere keeps its face readable; its separate painted surface rotates by travelled distance/radius. Only dash anticipation and brief impact compression change its shape; visual timers stop when the fixed simulation stops. A small skid lives in the renderer. No reference character design, library setup or numeric world scale reused.
