## 5. Tech
Vite + TypeScript + Three.js + pnpm. Deterministic fixed-step sim. **Ritmo for the sim only if** a clean-install smoke test passes (move, dash, collide, replay, draw); else own small fixed-step sim. Pin versions.
`src/content` (data) · `src/sim` (headless, seeded RNG, no DOM/three) · `src/input` (devices → tick commands) · `src/presentation` (three, materials, camera, FX, looks) · `src/ui` (DOM/CSS) · `src/app`.
Presentation never mutates sim state. Hit-stop/slow-mo = explicit time policy with input buffering. Browser host may use clocks; the sim may not.
Fast-feel budget: 60 fps target on phone; quality tiers; particle budget; shader warm-up; no full-screen bloom on low tier.
