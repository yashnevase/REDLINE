# STORY.md — REDLINE story bible (v2, 2026-10-30)
> Aligned with `SLICE_LOCKED_DECISIONS.md` §3. Names are placeholders. Story is told by art and a few lines: **comic cards ≤10 s, skippable, no voice.**

## 1. Premise [LOCKED]
A giant **Engine** stalled at its **redline** long ago. Six cultures grew around its broken pieces. **The Core** is the one piece still running, a spare that wakes up. Each boss holds a piece of the Engine: you bolt it on and gain its ability, until you reach the **Throne Engine** and restart it.
**Lore wording (PROPOSED):** the Core is the Engine's spare flywheel bearing. Because it is hardened metal, enemy blades chip and snap against it; the Blade you win is forged from the Core's own metal, so unlike enemy blades it does not snap.

## 2. Tone and rules
Loud, fearless, funny-tough, never gory. Enemies power down or are knocked out; nothing dies. Text ≤8 words per bubble. The Core never speaks: eyes only.
