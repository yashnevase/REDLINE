# PART B — Scrap-Rex v2 and difficulty (1 session)
**B1 Boss design rules (apply to every chapter boss):** 4 phases, 5–7 distinct attacks with ≥0.8 s telegraphs, attacks chain in later phases (shorter recovery, never shorter than telegraph minima), always a learnable **punish window** (×2 damage), an arena twist per phase, adds that matter, a clear **weak-spot** mechanic, no timers. Target: a new player on Normal loses 1–3 times before winning (first-attempt win ≤35 %), experienced players win in 2–3 min; retry < 2 s; death shows the killing attack.
**B2 Scrap-Rex v2 attacks:** (1) **Saw Sweep** wide arc (dash through the gap); (2) **Slam** with ring shock + falling scrap circles; (3) **Boomerang Saws** thrown discs on curved return paths; (4) **Roll Charge** (he becomes a ball, bounces off walls, stuns himself 1 s on impact = punish); (5) **Trophy Barrage** telegraphed falling debris zones; (6) **Vent Eruption** (vents burst in a sequence, arena lights flash red); (7) **Summon Grinders** (2 adds, weak to Stomp). Phase 1: 1–3. Phase 2: +4 and adds (Sawlets). Phase 3: +5, +6, hazards. Phase 4 enrage: chains of two attacks, +7, arena lights red, faster recoveries (≥ telegraph minima). Exposed plates after heavy attacks remain the weak-spot loop. Rex HP/phase thresholds retuned by bot so skilled bots win ~50 % on Normal.
**B3 Difficulty system (applies to all missions):** Normal stays the default and is slightly tougher than now: Mission 3 and Boss are the first real tests (bots should lose sometimes, not always). Redline = elites + surges + denser boss. Add **per-mission "mastery" goals** (no-hit, perfect dodges, style rank REDLINE) with stars. Fairness bots: Normal skilled bot 70–90 % mission clear, 40–60 % boss clear; report per difficulty.
**B4 Satisfying finish:** keep R4 kill ladder; add boss kill cam (slow-mo, camera pull-out, explosion chain, trophy).
**Gate:** owner plays Rex on Normal: it feels hard but fair; clip of all phases; bot win rates reported.

# PART C — Chapter 2: **Bog Moon / Hoppers** (pre-production now, build after acceptance)
**Why this chapter next:** it matches the owner's idea (enemies that leap away so melee chases fail), forces a meaningful **part choice** (Blade ranged and Stomp area-control are answers; the base Core can still win with skill, but is clearly harder), and introduces **Legs**. Reorder of the six chapters: **1 Foundry (Blade) → 2 Bog Moon/Hoppers (Frog legs) → 3 Cloud Docks/Skyfarers (Wing) → 4 Magma Forge/Hammerkin (Fist) → 5 Crystal Quarry/Facets (Spike) → 6 Storm Reactor/Arc-Choir (Coil) → Throne Engine finale.**
Chapter twists (fresh mechanics per chapter, no copy of Chapter 1): 2 hop-and-dodge enemies + slowing bog pools; 3 wind gusts push everyone, narrow decks, flying kites; 4 timed crushers and heat zones; 5 reflective beams and shatter glass; 6 conductive floor with chain lightning and overload; finale combines all.
**C1 Theme:** void stays purple; **world = toxic teal/acid green, mossy stone, dark swamp water, warm amber lamps**; props: lily-pad platforms, drum totems, mossy ruins, floating logs, glowing spores, fireflies, mist; background: giant dormant frog statues, hanging vines, bubbling pools; sparks replaced by spores/bubbles and electric-green flickers; audio pack: drums, croaks, water, wet hits.
**C2 Roster (Hoppers, START values):** *Skipper* (small, hit-and-run: hops in, strikes, **hops away** after attacking; landing ring telegraph 0.8 s; recovery 0.6 s = punish ×2); *Croaker* (ranged: drum pulse rings that expand; sidestep gaps); *Bogbulb* (leaves slow-bog pools, explodes into spores when popped; Stomp clears pools); *Tongue Lasher* (pulls the Core toward it on a telegraphed line; dash through cuts it); *Heavy Toad* (slow, giant leap, huge landing zone = heavy damage if caught, long recovery); *Elites* (armoured, double hop). RPS: melee-only chasing is weak; **Blade projectiles, Stomp landing control and Pulse Bash timing** are answers.
**C3 Boss — Bogtyrant Gulp:** huge frog-like orb with leg parts; hop-away kiting across the arena; **Leap Slam** (landing zone + shockwave), **Tongue Lash** (line pull), **Gulp** (swallows a ball-sized hazard and spits a barrage), **Drum Call** (summons Croakers; ring pulses), **Bog Flood** (water spreads, safe islands shrink), phase 4 enrage: double leaps. Weak spot: belly after Leap Slam. Same boss rules (B1).
**C4 Reward part: Frog Legs (Legs slot, X):** **Leap Slam** replaces Stomp: aim-line jump (up to 8 u, 0.5 s air, invulnerable while airborne), landing shockwave scaling with distance, short stun; plus **Hop-dash**: a second tap of Dash in air = mini-hop over obstacles/enemies; Space dash remains. Equip/unequip in the workshop like Blade; parts data-driven (`parts.ts`). (This honours the owner's "jump replaces dash" idea without removing the base dash.)
**C5 Missions:** 3 missions + boss with score targets scaled up (e.g. 14k/30k/50k), new twist per mission (M1 Skippers/Croakers, M2 +Bogbulbs/slow pools, M3 +Lashers/Heavy Toads); Blade recommended; cosmetics from trophies.
**C6 Build plan:** chapter config file, Look Bible colour script for the chapter, enemy models via the same match loop against owner images, new SFX/music pack mapping, fairness bots per difficulty, tests, parity pairs for owner approval, CHAPTER_PIPELINE checklist.
